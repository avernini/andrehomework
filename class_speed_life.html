Step 1: Scenario Hook & Warm-up (5-8 min)

Navigating the Pace of Modern Life

As an academic researcher or graduate student, managing time and digital distractions is a daily challenge. To discuss modern societal issues effectively, you need strong opinion phrases (*I tend to think..., From my perspective...*) and frequency expressions. Let's start with our warm-up discussion prompts:

Warm-up Questions (5 Critical Thinking Prompts)

💬 1. Do you feel that modern life moves too fast compared to previous decades?

💬 2. How many hours a day do you spend on digital devices and notifications?

💬 3. In your opinion, does constant connectivity help or hurt deep academic research?

💬 4. Do you actively try to disconnect ("offline time"), or is it too difficult?

💬 5. What is the main cause of mental fatigue for students today?

ACADEMIC DEBATE · ARTHUR & MATTHEW

Arthur: I tend to think that social media notifications destroy our daily concentration.

Matthew: I see your point, but constant connectivity also accelerates research collaboration.

Arthur: True, yet people rarely disconnect, which leads to severe burnout during graduate studies.

Step 3: Grammar Calibration (10-12 min)

Grammar Focus: Expressing Opinions & Hedging Language

In academic and intellectual discussions, we rarely sound 100% absolute. We use hedging expressions and viewpoint markers to state arguments professionally:

1. Viewpoint & Stance Markers

Framing your perspective:

• From my perspective, digital overload affects focus.

• It seems to me that people work too many hours.

• As far as I'm concerned, offline time is essential.

2. Softening Claims (Hedging)

Avoiding absolute statements:

• Technology tends to distract students.

• This issue might cause high levels of stress.

• Excessive notifications often lead to mental fatigue.

Fossilized Error Guard (Precision Check)
❌ COMMON MISTAKE

"From my point of view I think that..." (redundant phrasing)

"Technology is cause stress." (missing verb structure)

🟢 CORRECT STRUCTURE

"From my perspective, I think..." or "I believe..."

"Technology causes stress" or "tends to cause..."

Interactive Grammar Training (4 Challenges)

1. "______ my perspective, constant notifications damage productivity." (Viewpoint marker)

2. "Smartphones ______ to isolate people even in crowded rooms." (Softening tendency verb)

3. "It ______ to me that universities should promote digital detox." (Impersonal opinion structure)

4. "As far ______ I'm concerned, deep reading requires offline hours." (Prepositional phrase)
